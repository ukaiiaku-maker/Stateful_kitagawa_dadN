`v9_four_class_stateful_PD_shared_root_m1_marked_embryo_v1`.  Its one global

## Executable qualification

The focused tests establish raw-rate m=1 action, canonical m=3 action and signed
MPZ parity, candidate-count-independent global action, equal colocated mark
splitting, fail-closed empty support, separate/restorable hazard and mark RNGs,
transactional proposals, ordered within-phase localization, and no K=2 gate in
the m=1 audit contract.

A real 2000 MPa Peak trajectory exposed a downstream orchestration leak: a
caller cap of 10,000 rather than 100,000 cycles left the global attempt, mark,
stabilization, and first-softening coordinates identical, but shifted front
capture by about 0.5%.  The endpoint-consistent models now use a versioned
authoritative macro-transaction ceiling of 100,000 cycles.  Repeating the same
>10x caller-partition comparison then gave byte-identical final PD arrays,
signed MPZ summary, event ledger, and all endpoint coordinates.

A hash-verified restart from the accepted boundary at N=566, before the marked
crossing, reproduced the attempt and stabilization coordinates and selected
site exactly.  Hazard action, threshold and both stochastic streams were
preserved.  Sparse FEM reconstruction produces a 33 micro-Pa difference in the
localized local stress and a 1.1e-6-cycle difference at the much later front
capture (relative 1.9e-12); this is bounded floating-point reconstruction, not
an RNG/state-ledger difference.  Accordingly, stochastic identity is exact and
continuous fields are restart-equivalent to roundoff; byte identity across a
fresh sparse-factorization reconstruction is not claimed.

## MPZ-to-FEM relaxation audit

At the captured endpoints, the signed MPZ response is far too small to justify
adding a new two-way relaxation law:

| sigma_a (MPa) | emitted line content | unsigned accumulated slip | max backstress | signed shielding | conservative strain bound | E*strain bound |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2000 | 5.244e-12 | 5.244e-12 | 97.6 Pa | -1.16e-68 Pa sqrt(m) | 7.18e-16 | 2.95e-4 Pa |
| 1500 | 1.125e-9 | 1.125e-9 | 1.43 kPa | -1.25e-75 Pa sqrt(m) | 1.54e-13 | 0.0632 Pa |

The strain column is a deliberately conservative dimensional projection
`b * unsigned_line_content / (2 um source-zone length)`, not a newly accepted
constitutive coupling.  It is consistent with the directly computed negligible
backstress/shielding and continuum `epsp_max` below 5e-19.  Even its elastic
stress bound is immaterial relative to 6.8--9.0 GPa root stresses.  Therefore
two-way MPZ-to-FEM relaxation is not activated merely to lower the stress.

## Scope and conclusion

This milestone establishes the ideal smooth-macronotch Peak m=1 baseline,
including a finite attempt, stabilized seed, front capture, and a materially
lower VHCF condition.  It does not establish an endurance limit: one 1500 MPa
attempt realization and four transition/mark paths are finite-horizon evidence.
No other material class or microflaw branch was started.  A future two-scale
geometry study must use independently justified flaw dimensions and must not
double-apply Inglis and K-radius amplification.
